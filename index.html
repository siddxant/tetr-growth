Where each channel landed

Does it repeat without starting from zero, and does it build trust or only reach? Position is qualitative.

The starting point

A global programme that the world read as a local one

Tetr was designed as a global institution from day one: students rotate across campuses in several countries and build real businesses as part of the programme. But in our first year, the market did not see that. We were founded from India, our early team was in India, and to a parent in Texas or a counsellor in Munich, that was the whole story.

Credibility is the whole product in higher education. Families are making a four-year, high-cost decision about a brand with no alumni, no rankings and no campus they could visit. Every channel we tried had to answer one question first: why should I trust you?

Perception

Geography defined the brand

"Indian institution" came before "global programme" in every first conversation, regardless of what the curriculum actually was.

Proof

No proof points yet

No graduates, no outcomes, no peer references. Only our own claims, which is the weakest form of evidence a family can be given.

Channels

Paid reach without trust

Ads could get attention in the US and Europe, but attention without credibility converted into questions, not applications.

What we tried

Six channels, tested honestly, four kept

We ran every channel long enough to learn whether it compounded. The ones that built trust stayed. The ones that only rented attention were cut or repositioned.

What worked

The B2B and word-of-mouth flywheel

Credibility is borrowed before it is earned. A counsellor who recommends Tetr lends us their trust with the family. A student who does well lends us theirs with the next cohort. The platform work was to make that loop repeat on its own.

1,600+partner schools across 115 countries
65+NPS among partner schools, driving repeat referrals
42%of students come from the same community, family or school as earlier cohorts
8sibling pairs have chosen Tetr; team grew from 8 to 23 to run the engine

The mechanics are simple and slow: an on-ground team member builds a relationship with a counsellor, one family applies, that student does well, and the counsellor recommends again next year with more confidence. Once a school sends its first student, it rarely stops.

Taking Tetr to the world

Creators tell the story in their language. Students tell it in theirs.

Two kinds of content did what paid posts could not: creators embedded in the programme for long-form pieces, and students documenting their own journey on their own channels. Both carry trust because the audience already follows the person, not the brand.

How perception changed

Three years, three different conversations

YEAR 1 · PROVE WE EXIST

Reach without trust

"Is this an Indian college?"
  • Broad performance campaigns and one-off influencer posts in the US and Europe
  • High attention, low conversion: every lead needed the credibility conversation from scratch
  • Started the first counsellor relationships by hand, city by city
YEAR 2 · PROVE IT WORKS

Borrowed credibility

"A counsellor I trust mentioned you."
  • Built the B2B team and the school network as the primary channel
  • Offline events in priority cities so families could meet students and the team
  • First student ventures and outcomes became the proof we had been missing
YEAR 3 · PROVE WE'RE GLOBAL

Earned credibility

"Which campus are they in this term?"
  • Dedicated US and China go-to-market teams with local content ecosystems
  • Long-form creator partnerships in 10 languages and students publishing their own journeys
  • Performance marketing re-entered as a long-term brand engine on top of proof

Attention is not credibility

Paid and influencer reach got us seen in the US and Europe. It did not get us believed. Trust had to be borrowed from counsellors and earned through outcomes first.

Cut what doesn't repeat

One-off influencer posts reset to zero every time. Counsellor relationships and embedded creator partnerships compound. We moved budget and headcount to the channels that remember us.

Go local before going loud

Priority markets only moved once they had their own teams, content and trusted voices. Performance marketing works now because there is proof underneath it.
